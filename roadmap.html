Rollout Roadmap

  1. Phase I Year 1

    Seeking People

    Use media to adapt individual first. Deploy in priority regions with transparent metrics. Guage first public response to non-attached media.

    • Bias testing & feedback
    • Community onboarding sessions
  2. Phase II Year 2

    Seeking Scale

    Expand footprint; improve performance and equity benchmarks with real-world data.

    • Expand to new partner sites
    • Performance & fairness testing
    • External audit of AI models
  3. Phase III Year 3

    Seeking Partnership

    Standardize ethical frameworks across partners; publish audits and outcomes. Partnership groups.

    • Network-wide governance playbook
    • Annual transparency report
    • AI & Community advisory council
