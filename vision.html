Our Vision at MedNova

Vision Statement

“To make equitable, AI-powered chronic disease management accessible to every patient - urban or rural - within the next three years.”

Our Bold Goal.

Launch MedNova's AI-integrated wearable platform in three pilot regions (Texas, Ontario, and British Columbia) by 2026, improving care efficiency by 25% and reducing hospital readmissions by 15%.

Courageous decision.

We chose public healthcare first - the toughest, most regulated market - because leadership means tackling challenges that matter most.
