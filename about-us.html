Plan how you will fit the Dalyell curriculum to your degree.

There are three elements to consider when planning how to fit the Dalyell curriculum to your degree:

  1. Compulsory Dalyell units (Table D) requirements
  2. Optional acceleration and enrichment options
  3. A global mobility experience

The Interdisciplinary Studies Handbook explains compulsory and optional curriculum requirements and lists Dalyell units and enrichment units.

Each Dalyell Faculty has a handbook to understand your degree requirements.

Understand there is a Cross-Semester Average Mark (CSAM) requirement of 75

The CSAM period includes all semesters required to complete at least 48 credit points of units but can include more than 48 credit points. For example, if you study 18 credit points each semester, your CSAM period will run over three semesters and include 54 credit points of units. Once a unit has been counted in a CSAM period, it won't be counted in the next CSAM period.

For more information on CSAM, visit the University Website

If you are struggling to maintain a CSAM of 75 due to extenuating circumstances, contact your Faculty Lead as soon as possible to discuss your situation. You may be asked to show why you should remain in the stream and provide supporting documentation. Your Faculty Lead can explain the process.

Remember extenuating circumstances are situations that are unforeseeable and beyond your reasonable control, and include unexpected, serious or long-term illness or misadventure that is impacting on your ability to engage appropriately with your studies. You can also experience extenuating circumstances despite accessing the appropriate support systems available at the University and resulting adjustments, for example special consideration and arrangements or Inclusion and Disability Services.

The Student Life, Wellbeing and Support website has everything you need to know about the services, resources and events you can access throughout your degree when you need extra help.
